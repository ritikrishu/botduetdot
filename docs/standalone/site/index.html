BotDuetDot

A self-hosted command center for coding agents. Use Claude Code, Codex and other supported agents with your own subscriptions. Arc-style Spaces keep always-on bot threads separate from development work.

The macOS app runs your server. Android remotely controls it over LAN or Tailscale and requires pairing with a reachable server. Create and edit Spaces on desktop or web, then switch them on mobile.

macOS releases (Apple silicon) · Google Play listing (availability depends on release approval)

No BotDuetDot account, T3 Connect or push notifications. Analytics is off by default. Provider subscriptions and charges are separate.
